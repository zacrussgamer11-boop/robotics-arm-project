# Build Notes - All .STL files can be found in the CAD folder

## Version 1
    Plan - One servo moving a single arm forward and backwards being controlled by a thumbstick

    ## Servo Horn
        - Screw hole diameter: 1.1mm
        - Peice width: 1.5mm
        - Using stock plastic horn
        - 3D - Printed arm will use
        - Tinkercad used for first prototype

    ## Base
        Base V1
        - This will mount the arduino on a flat bed, make a 90* wall and then attach the servo onto that
        - Housing for breadboard too small, when trying to back the servo into its mount the space was too small, entire thing warped, and was incorrectly printed
        Base V1.1
        - Hoping it goes better lol, made sure the breadboard could fit this time, made the entire plate thicker so it wont warp as much or at all, made the inlets deeper so the electronics seat better + added a hole to connect usbc to the project, I also made the screw holes .1mm tinier so the thread screws can tap better when mounting the servo, I also removed any unnessisarry overhangs as they lead to print errors and surface issues.
        Base V1.2
        - Bread board and arduino properly fit allowing room also for their plug ins.
        - Need the screw holes to mount the servo a tiny bit tinier but right now it friction fits on but I want to assure a stable mount
        Base V1.3
        - Smaller screw holes.
