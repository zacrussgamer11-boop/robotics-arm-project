    ## Circuit V1
        ## IR Reciever
        - In the circuit diagram it uses a different model of IR, it is meant as representation of my circuit.
        - My Physical module uses G = GND, R = 5V, Y = signal (D8). Frontfacing the GND needs to be left most wire, PWR (5V) needs to be middle, and out needs to be most right. (If you are using this as a framework please check independently your own specific format)
        ## Servo
        - On the diagram the Signal and PWR terminals are swapped aswell, again please check your own framework and what connection points you have yourself.
